### 🔬 Now, let's get to know each other better!

Let me start...

Thank you for visiting my GitHub profile!

As someone who deeply loves science and knowledge, and enjoys working on projects from A to Z, I have found myself involved in multiple sectors of engineering, and I see all of them as parts of one big solution.

Science is not made up of completely separate fields. There are only specific laws and principles that govern the universe. To enable ourselves to understand the world and find solutions to our problems, we need to acquire that knowledge.

And whenever we acquire it, we discover two things.

**First**, it becomes impossible to limit ourselves to one specific, narrow specialization while aiming to find real engineering solutions. That definitely doesn't mean we need to study all of engineering and scientific knowledge to solve a problem. Instead, it means choosing a specialization and developing a deeper understanding of the fields surrounding it. This is especially useful for my approach to building comprehensive projects.

**Secondly**, to be real, when we understand the fundamental laws of nature, that alone unlocks a lot of engineering fields for us. Those who genuinely love this art will be able to perceive all its different facets as different points of view of the same core solution.

💡 Now, that was a quick overview of my POV on engineering.
